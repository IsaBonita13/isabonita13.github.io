About Me

Digital Marketing • Event Coordination

Where many see digital marketing and event production as separate worlds, I build at their intersection. The most meaningful brand experiences don’t stop at the screen. They spark curiosity online, translate into memorable moments in person, and cultivate lasting community long after the final applause.

My background bridges data-informed digital strategy with hands-on operational precision. Whether I'm managing vendor ecosystems, attendee registration workflows in Cvent, and on-site logistics for regional business conferences, or designing luxury e-commerce platforms, optimizing SEO, and producing engaging short-form video, I bring calm focus and obsessive attention to detail to fast-paced environments.

As President of Wright State’s American Marketing Association chapter and an Emerging Marketing Leaders scholar, I lead with energy, empathy, and a people-first mindset. From steering cross-functional teams to launching integrated multi-channel campaigns, I’m driven by work that moves people to action.
